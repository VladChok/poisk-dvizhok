# Research Method

**Status:** bootstrap skeleton

This is the minimum operational shape of a research assignment and handoff used by the Research Engine. The stable responsibility contract for the Research role lives in Structura `docs/roles/RESEARCH.md`; this file must not become a second conflicting role definition. It is intentionally incomplete. Real research tasks should test the shape before additional process or abstractions are added.

## QUESTION

What specific question must the research answer?

## WHY IT MATTERS

Which decision, risk, or piece of work depends on the answer?

## SCOPE

What is included, excluded, and time-bounded?

## WHAT TO STUDY

Which systems, repositories, documents, claims, or workflows need inspection?

## EVIDENCE NEEDED

What sources and observable facts would support an answer? Record source identity, date or version, and relevant limitations.

## STOP CONDITION

What result, evidence threshold, or constraint tells the researcher to stop?

## RESULT / LIMITATIONS

What was found? Which parts are facts, which are interpretations, and what remains unknown?
